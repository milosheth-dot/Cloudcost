Understand the estimate

kgCO₂e = IT energy (kWh) × PUE × grid intensity (gCO₂e/kWh) ÷ 1,000. Imported energy and intensity must be supplied by you. Estimates exclude embodied hardware emissions. Billing alone cannot determine energy consumption.

No live provider APIs, renewable-energy claims, or composite Green Score are included in this MVP. Periods end on the dataset’s latest observed day; missing days remain unknown.
